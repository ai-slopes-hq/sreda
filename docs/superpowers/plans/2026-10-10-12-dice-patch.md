# DICE a whole patch — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans.

**Goal:** DICE at the top right replaces the canvas with a random playable patch, rhythmic or ambient (spec rev. 29).

**Architecture:** `packages/core/src/factory/dicepatch.ts` — `dicePatch(specs: Specs, seed: number): Doc`, built with
the factory `build()` (real units, cables, frames with clocks, STACK packing) and the core generators (`genNotes`,
`euclid`, `SCALES`, `mulberry32`). The UI's top-right NEW becomes DICE (`s.openProject`-like replace: one undo step,
fit, fade in, unnamed project).

## Global Constraints

- English UI copy (`DICE`); one undo step; no samples needed (GRAINS only on LIVE).
- Every seed: a valid document; `wireProblems` empty of "sound does not reach any OUT" and empty inputs; rendering
  4 s (`renderDoc`) gives a master RMS between −40 and −3 dBFS, peak ≤ 0 dBFS after the limiter, all finite.
