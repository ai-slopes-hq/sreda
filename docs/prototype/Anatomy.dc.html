CABLE AND JACK COLOUR = ROLE
AUDIO · nominal ±1 · stacked cables sum
MOD · ±1 or 0…1 · attenuverter on the input
PITCH · +1 = one octave up · attenuverter on the input
TRIGGER · fires rising above 0.5 · ratio on the input · anything periodic is a clock

1
NAME
Drag anywhere on the module to move it. Hover and press R to randomise its knobs.
2
INPUT · STACKED
Any number of cables; audio and CV sum, triggers merge. Drag from it to pick up the newest cable, or into empty space to add a module.
3
NORMAL
Dashed ring: pre-wired inside the module (here CV ← the ping envelope) until a real cable goes in. Hover names the source.
4
ATTENUVERTER
Under every CV input label, −1…+1. Drag, wheel, double-click resets. Dim while at its default.
5
RATIO
Under every trigger input label, ÷16…×8, applied after the merge. A clock at ×2 here doubles it for this input only.
6
KNOB + MOD JACK
Drag (Shift = fine), wheel, double-click resets. Patch the corner jack to modulate: the ring is its attenuverter (Alt-drag), the dot is where the knob is now.
7
OPTION
Two values: click toggles. More (▾): click opens a list. Wheel steps. Waves, scales and stage modes are drawn as glyphs. No mod jack.
8
OUTPUT + LEVEL
Any number of cables. Click to hear only this output. The bar is its held peak.
